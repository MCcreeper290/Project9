Project 9 Version 2.0

Version 2.0 of Project 9 is the most powerful version yet. V2 is made of redstone energy which allows it to be self-sustaining. While V2 is now stable any damage even the slightest could cause it to become extremly unstable. For this reason a specilized system has been made that can safely shutdown itself if it detects damage. Incase the emergency shutdown system fails we have put V2 in a underground blast proof room.

Pros

  • High Energy Output
  • Self-sustaining

Cons

  • It can become Extremly Unstable if it is damaged.

Uses

The V2 is ment to power high energy demanding machines and devices like the shield system which at most only uses 5% of the total energy. Its estimated that 1% of the total energy produced in a second could power the whole earth for a day.

This is a photo of V2. In the center we have the actual core itself made mostly from redstone blocks with a diameter of 33 meters. To the sides we have long massive cords designed to draw large amounts of energy if needed. They also are used to input settings into the reactor, in the center of the cords we have pipes to transfer coolant into the reactor to keep the temperatures stable.

What if it fails

While the chance of a failure is low there are two things that could happen. The first type causes a major damage with a radius of 100 miles and a fallout zone of 200 miles however this is with the blast proof room. This type happens 99.5% of the time. The second type is when the core collapses in on itself. Then extreme density of the redstone creates a black hole which would destroy everything around it. However this happens only 0.5% of the time which is a 1 in 200 chance.
