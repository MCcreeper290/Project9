Shield System

The Shield System is a defense mechanism that when activated puts up a indestructable dome of any size.

The shield system is powered by project 9 V2 and at most uses 5% of the total energy of V2.

Pros

  • Unbreakable

Cons

  • Demands lots of power

Problems

The only way the shield could be damaged/broken is if something with more power than V2 is used against the shield.

-The Project 9 Team
